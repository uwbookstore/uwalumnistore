Our Commitment to Accessibility

The Wisconsin Foundation and Alumni Association is committed to providing an accessible and inclusive online experience for all users of the UW Alumni Store website.

This website is operated in partnership with the University Book Store and is powered by third-party technologies. We strive to apply accessibility best practices and to support the principles of the World Wide Web Consortium's Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (version 2.2, level AA) wherever practical and technically feasible. Because certain aspects of the platform are managed by third-party providers, some accessibility limitations may exist.

We are continually working with our partners to improve the accessibility and usability of the UW Alumni Store. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, experience difficulty using any part of this website, or require assistance accessing products, services, or information, please contact us. We will make reasonable efforts to provide an alternative means of access and to address accessibility concerns when possible.
