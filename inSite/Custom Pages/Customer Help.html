Non-Tech Return Policy

If you are not completely satisfied with any product, we will gladly replace it or refund the purchase price of the item.

A non-tech item may be returned any time if it was purchased from a University Book Store location or website and is in saleable condition. Returns without a receipt will be refunded at the lowest price ever sold and credited to a University Book Store gift card. For additional information, please contact us at 800-993-2665, extension 1848.

Please do the following:

  • Items must be in new condition with original packaging and accessories.
  • Circle the item(s) on the packing list and note whether you want an EXCHANGE or CREDIT.
  • If an exchange, please note the size and/or color you want.

Tech Return Policy

Tech items may be returned within 15 days only with receipt and in new, saleable condition, complete with all packaging and pieces.

An unopened product will be refunded in full, and an opened product will be subject to a 15% restocking fee, with the following exceptions:

  • Open batteries, graphing calculators, in-ear headphones, ink/toner, printers, and storage devices are not returnable.
  • Special order and sale items are generally not returnable – ask for details before purchasing please.
  • Defective items may require warranty processing or may be exchanged for the same item after verified defective, per manufacturer and store policies.

Clearance Item Policy

You will not be charged for your order until the order ships.

We search for clearance items at each of our four locations, so it may take longer for those items to be pulled. If you are placing a Next Day Air or 2nd Day Air order, the order processing time will be delayed while we check all our locations for the discontinued item.

If we don’t find the item, your order packing slip will show it as “Discontinued” and you will not receive that item.

A credit card is required for all orders. When an order is placed on our system no payments are collected until we ship the order. Our system does check with your credit/debit card provider to make sure that there are sufficient funds available to cover the total amount of the order (your financial institution might list this fund check with a “Pending” notation). That happens even if you have entered a gift card with a balance that covers the entire cost of the order. When your order is shipped, our system will apply the gift card first. If that covers the total amount of the order, your credit/debit card will not be billed. If the gift card covers a portion of the order total, your credit/debit card will be billed for the difference. Only if the gift card has no balance would the entire amount of the order be billed to your credit/debit card.

“Pick up at store” orders have the same processing time because they are filled at our warehouse. You will receive notification when your order is ready for pickup.
